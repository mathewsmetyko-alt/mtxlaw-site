Why The Metyko Law Firm

Not Every Lawyer Is Built the Same

There are hundreds of attorneys in San Antonio. Most have never run a business, never led soldiers in combat, never stood in front of a jury and delivered. We have.

Battle-Tested Under Pressure

Mathews Metyko served as a U.S. Army officer in Operation Enduring Freedom — making high-stakes decisions in conditions most attorneys will never experience. That composure, discipline, and commitment to the mission carries directly into how we litigate. When opposing counsel pushes, we don't fold.

MBA Precision in Every Case

As a serial entrepreneur and MBA, Mathews understands what actually drives value — in a business dispute, a contract negotiation, or a damages calculation. He knows which numbers matter, which clauses are dealbreakers, and how to frame your case in terms that resonate with jurors, judges, and opposing counsel alike.

You Get the Attorney — Not a Paralegal

At large firms, you hire a name and get passed to whoever is available. At The Metyko Law Firm, Mathews personally handles your case from intake to resolution. You have his direct contact. He knows your file. When you call, he answers. That level of access isn't common — and it makes a difference.

Tried to Verdict — Not Just Settled

Many attorneys settle every case because trial is expensive, uncomfortable, and risky for them. Mathews has tried cases to jury verdict and knows how to prepare one properly from day one. Insurance companies and opposing counsel know the difference between a lawyer who settles and one who tries. It affects every offer you receive.

Deep Trucking Industry Knowledge

Commercial trucking litigation is a specialty within a specialty. We represent both injured victims and trucking companies — which means we know both sides of every argument, every FMCSA regulation, every insurance tactic, and every defense strategy. That dual-sided knowledge is rare and gives our clients a significant edge.

No Fee Unless We Win

Personal injury cases are handled on a contingency fee basis — meaning you pay nothing unless we recover for you. No upfront costs. No hourly bills. Our interests are completely aligned with yours: we win when you win.

Practice Areas

Business Litigation

Contract Review & Negotiations
